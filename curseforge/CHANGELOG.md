# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release (beta). The same changelog applies to every file: Fabric and NeoForge, Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 26.1.2 and 1.21.1.

### Classes and skills
- Five classes: Warrior, Rogue, Ranger, Mage and Summoner, each with a skill ladder out to level 100
- Seven Mage elements (fire, ice, lightning, wind, earth, light, dark), each with its own spells and a level 100 form
- Levels, five stats, mana, and a hold-to-open skill wheel
- Skill proficiency: ranks 1 to 10 lower mana cost, raise damage, lengthen duration and speed up casting

### Combat
- Timed parry, dodge with invulnerability frames, posture, finishers and stamina
- Monsters telegraph their heavy attacks

### Gates and the Spire
- Ranked gates E to S that leak monsters until the guardian is slain, with a collapse countdown afterwards
- Gates appear around every player, and often near villages
- Finished gate dungeons are wiped back to bedrock
- The Spire: a procedural tower of 25 to 60 floors with six themed bands, rest floors, guardian floors and a final boss
- Mana crystals that are very hard to break until their floor's guardian is dead, and custom monsters for every band

### Echoes and items
- Bind defeated monsters as Echoes, sort them into groups, and give Guard, Attack, Seek or Guide orders
- Gate Shards, Mana Potions, Echo Shards and the Gate Leave Scroll (craft it from an Ender Pearl and Paper)
- Compound Bow with a grappling hook, Quiver, and graves
- Two NPC traders in the tower hall

### Multiplayer and commands
- Parties: `/gatebound party invite | accept | leave | kick | list`, open to all players
- All other `/gatebound` commands are operator-only
- Works in singleplayer and on servers; install on both client and server
